# Code of conduct

ConfigPilot welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Be respectful, patient, and specific in code review and discussion.
Help people understand the reasoning behind a suggestion and address the work
without attacking the person.

Project spaces include issues, pull requests, reviews, and other channels maintained
for ConfigPilot. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, personal attacks,
unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information
are unacceptable. Do not share credentials or private configuration as part of a
discussion or conduct report.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, or
restrict participation based on the behavior, severity, and repeated incidents.
They should explain decisions where doing so does not expose private information
and apply these expectations consistently, including to maintainers.

To request a private conduct-reporting channel, open an
[issue](https://github.com/ghazy001/configpilot/issues) titled **Request private
maintainer contact** without names, allegations, or private details. Share details
only after a private channel has been agreed. There is no published response-time
commitment or independent appeals body at this development stage. For a software
vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
